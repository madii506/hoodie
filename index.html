FAQ

Questions from inside the hoodie.

Who is inside the hoodie?

Nobody. Then you. The mascot is an empty hoodie by design — it's whoever is holding it. The dev is also a hoodie.

Is the mint real right now?

Not yet. The site is in preview mode: mints, purchases and the wallet are simulated in your browser so you can see exactly how it works. Your minted hoodies and items are saved locally. The on-chain mint and marketplace open with the token launch, announced on our X.

Is the PFP generator uploading my picture?

No. It runs entirely in your browser with a canvas. Nothing leaves your device. Download the PNG and set it as your PFP.

How are fees paid in ETH?

The $HOODIE pool charges a creator fee on trades, collected in ETH and distributed to holders proportionally. No claiming, staking or selling required.

Where is the CA?

Posted on our X at launch and shown at the top of this page. Any CA anywhere else before that is fake. The hoodie does not DM.

Is this connected to Robinhood?

No. It's a hoodie. Robinhood Chain is a public network; $HOODIE is a memecoin on it, not affiliated with Robinhood in any way.
